## What is it?

My personal dotfiles for Arch Linux: a custom Hyprland setup with a clear **Cyan** color style. Full rice: Wofi menus, Waybar, Hyprlock, Fish + Starship, Kitty, LazyVim.

## In my setup

This was my daily driver until I moved my workflow to NiriWM (see the niri-dotfiles project). Repo stays up as an archive, hence dormant.

## Why did I build it?

Started **ricing** journey on Arch and **obsessed** with cyan style. Even though at that time I had a **private i3wm dotfiles**. LOL, I uses 2 tilling window manager at the time.

> I was still an amateur at the time, hihiii.

## What I learned

- **Hyprland Configuring:** keybinds handler, autostart, hyprlock, and hyprpaper.
- **Shell Script:** management/adjusdments `.sh` to (waybar and wofi) from [elifouts](https://github.com/elifouts/Dotfiles) and [dln (wofi-emoji)](https://github.com/dln/wofi-emoji)
- **Some lesson about Hyprland:** such as config error popup, waybar tooltip crashes hyprland, and something hyprland crash after update. Yeahh, debugging and fixing.

## Why I moved to Niri?

> because in Niri I can focus more on workflows, development and minimize breaking changes/problems when updating system (Window Manager). But this project `aadnanmt/hyprland-dotfiles` is already dromant.
